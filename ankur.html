Notice

TeacherAnkur
"You are the sensor; Ankur is the scaffold."
  1. Notice: Tap what you saw: reading, writing, speaking, behaviour. (Teacher)
  2. Add the work: A photo of what the child made. (Teacher)
  3. One small target: Drafted from India's NCF-FS competencies. (Ankur drafts)
  4. Decide: Approve, edit, redo or skip. Nothing is automatic. (Teacher)
  5. Print the activity: Low-cost play with stones, bottle caps and chalk. (Teacher)
  6. Check and see growth: A month-end check. "Needs more time" is a normal answer. (Ankur tracks)

Research and structure

Mapping the flow against six other tools.

Six tools compared, and none covers all five things a teacher needs. Foreign tools have the right shape; Indian tools have the right curriculum. Switch between the views.

Decisions

Decisions I made about the AI.

  1. Approve, edit, redo or skip.A child is never labelled or scored.
  2. Nicknames only, data kept on the device.It lowers the bar for schools to trust it.
  3. Test scores and leaderboards left out on purpose.They would break the non-diagnostic rule.
  4. Not another content or testing app.DIKSHA and Jaadui Pitara are partners here.
