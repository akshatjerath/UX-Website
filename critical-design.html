Speculative design, team project, 2026

Grow below, keep above.

What if empty city buildings grew our food and kept it fresh, right where we live? "The Hub" is our speculative answer. Then we pushed it to 2043 and asked who holds the timer on that freshness.

Team
Akhil Mistry, Akshat Jerath, Sayee Patil
Studio
Critical Design
Methods
Futures timeline, waterfall, SWOT, scenario, 300 ideas
Format
Timeline poster, design brief, scenario
Status
Speculative. Ongoing
How we worked
Group project. We built every stage together: each of us pitched ideas for the timeline, then we polished and filtered them as a group. From the cards, each of us wrote 100 ideas, 300 in all, and we clustered them together.

Research

A futures timeline, 1920 to 2043.

Who decides how long food lasts, era by era. The clock follows you down.

1920year

Timer held byPhysics

  1. The past

    1920 – 1945

    Physics buys food time, and anyone with the machine can use it.

    • 1930Flash freezingKeeps the texture.
    • 1930sCold chainFridges on farms and trucks.
    • 1940sDehydrationMilk and egg ship as powder.

  2. The present

    2024 – 2026

    India grows enough but loses it on the road. Distance is the timer.

    • Our briefReuse buildingsGrow food and keep it fresh in one place.
    • InsightLoss is distanceClose the gap and the loss shrinks.
    • OpportunityCity food hubsDrying, earthen cool rooms, controlled air.

  3. Near future

    2027 – 2031

    Freshness stops being only about cold. The lab writes it into the seed and the coating.

    • 2027Edible coatingsLeave the lab for the market.
    • 2028CRISPR tomatoSellable for 36 days, up from 28.
    • 2031Cold storage gapAbout 33,000 reefer trucks of 62,000 needed.

  4. The selected sequence

    2033 – 2043

    Five points from our waterfall. Freshness becomes a number someone else sets.

    1. 2033
      AnalyticalEdible coatings become a labelled shelf-life standard, crop by crop.
    2. 2035
      IndustrialCold-chain build-out becomes a political flagship, still short of need.
    3. 2037
      NaturalWarming shortens natural shelf-life faster than coatings extend it.
    4. 2039
      Analytical · our year of interventionA digital twin of the cold chain forecasts loss before it happens.
    5. 2043
      AnalyticalRoom-temperature preservation ends the need for constant cold.

Redesign brief, after the SWOT

Blocking corporate control of India's food-freshness clock by 2039.

After a SWOT on all three scenarios, we picked the one where our skills could push back: AI, systems and reverse engineering.

Direction: avoidYear of intervention: 2039

  1. ProblemPlatforms and seed owners hold the freshness data.
  2. NeedFreshness data farmers can read, trust and contest.
  3. InsightWhoever writes the expiry clock owns the value.
  4. OpportunityAn open freshness standard with a forecast farmers can read.

Scenario, 2043

The era of adaptive freshness.

An algorithmic food system where AI breeding and digital-twin sensors manage produce down to the cell. Every harvest carries a software expiry clock that sets its market value in real time.
