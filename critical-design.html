Speculative design, team project, 2026

Grow below, keep above.

What if empty city buildings grew our food and kept it fresh, right where we live? “The Hub” is our speculative answer. Then we pushed it to 2045 and asked who holds the timer on that freshness.

  1. The past

    1920 – 1945

    Science learns to buy food time with physics: freeze it, heat it, dry it, add what's missing. Anyone with the machine can use the method.

    • 1924 – 1941FortificationIodized salt, vitamin-D milk, enriched flour.
    • 1928 – 1932Vitamin isolationVitamin C isolated, so you know what to add.
    • 1930Flash freezingBirdseye's quick-freeze keeps the texture.
    • 1930sPasteurizationHigh heat, short time, safe milk.
    • 1930sCold chainMechanical refrigeration and refrigerated transport.
    • 1940sDehydrationPowdered milk and egg ship light to the front.

  2. The present

    2024 – 2026

    India grows enough but loses it on the road. Here distance is the timer: every kilometre between farm and plate eats shelf-life.

    Our briefReusing buildings to grow and naturally keep food fresh

    InsightMost loss comes from distance. Grow and keep food in the same local building and the distance disappears.

    OpportunityEmpty city buildings become food hubs that grow regional produce and keep it fresh with drying, cool earthen storage and controlled air.

  3. Near future

    2027 – 2031

  4. The selected sequence

    2033 – 2043

    Five points picked from our waterfall, each adding to the world before it. Freshness turns into a number someone else can read and set.

    1. 2033
      AnalyticalEdible coatings become a labelled shelf-life standard, crop by crop.
    2. 2035
      IndustrialCold-chain build-out becomes a political flagship, still short of need.
    3. 2037
      NaturalWarming shortens natural shelf-life faster than coatings extend it.
    4. 2039
      AnalyticalA digital twin of the cold chain forecasts loss before it happens.
    5. 2043
      AnalyticalRoom-temperature preservation ends the need for constant cold.

Scenario, 2045

The era of adaptive freshness.

An algorithmic food system where AI breeding and digital-twin sensors manage produce down to the cell. Every harvest carries a software expiry clock that sets its market value in real time.
