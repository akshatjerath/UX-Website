How I work

Every project follows the same loop, and each step leaves something you can look at.

Research

Interviews, surveys and observation to understand what people do today and why.

Define

Turning findings into a clear problem, a persona and a goal that can be checked.

Ideate

Sketching flows and structures, then choosing the strongest direction.

Prototype

Building clickable screens in Figma to test ideas before committing to them.

Test

Putting the prototype in front of real users, then fixing what they struggle with.
