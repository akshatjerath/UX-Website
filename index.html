About me.

I'm doing an MDes in Design Futures at ATLAS SkillTech in Mumbai, and I volunteer as a game design instructor with University of the People.

I like starting where people already lose something, like money at a restaurant table or a child slipping through a busy class. Research first, then the smallest change that makes the choice clearer.
