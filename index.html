(About)

I teach design. I practise UX.

I'm in the first year of an MDes in Design Futures at ATLAS SkillTech University in Mumbai, and I'm on the faculty at École Intuit Lab.

I like starting where people are already losing something: money at a restaurant table, a child slipping through a busy classroom. Then I research it properly and design the smallest thing that makes the decision clearer.

Teaching keeps me honest about it. Most of my week is running sessions, giving feedback and explaining why one option beats another.
