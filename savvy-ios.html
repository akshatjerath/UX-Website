iOS · Platform design

SAVVY on iOS

The iOS version: night and day screens, the Dynamic Island hand-off, and a few extra screens.

Platform
iOS
Status
Interactive concept, sample data, not usability tested

Illustrative prototype calculation, not a live verified price

Platform principles

The iOS screens use night and day themes while keeping the price first, then what you keep, then the maths.

UI type: Inter. The price is always the biggest thing on the screen.

Key decisions and reasons

Teal replaces lime and a serif carries the price. The order stays the same: the price first, then what you keep, then the maths.

  1. One price first: the cash you pay tonight.
  2. What you keep, compared with just paying the bill.
  3. Coins sit on their own line and never change the order.
  4. One tap hands you to the cheapest app. SAVVY never pays.
iOS, version by version

How the iOS screen got here.

The same moment every time: comparing the apps for one bill. Five passes, from a list of apps to one answer.

  1. 01
    Every app in a list

    Four apps, cheapest first, with a Book or Pay switch on top.

  2. 02
    Back to grey

    A rough wireframe to test the walk-in case. The best option sits on top.

  3. 03
    Open the maths

    One app opens up: bill, card, what you pay at the table, coins later.

  4. 04
    Ranked by cash

    Three cards ranked by what you pay now. Coins get their own line.

  5. Final
    One answer

    One price at the top, then what you keep, then the maths.
